Ranking engine
01 · Multimodal similarity

Room image → CLIP image embedding → Qdrant nearest-neighbor retrieval.

02 · Hard constraints

Category, budget and optional room filters remove invalid candidates.

03 · Spatial fit

Product width/depth are checked against the available footprint plus clearance.

04 · Quality + value

Ratings, review volume and budget utilization affect the final ranking.

For exact real-world dimensions from a single photo, the user must provide a scale/reference or available footprint. The system never invents centimeter measurements.
